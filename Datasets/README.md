## Datasets allowing to plot the Figure 1 and to create the inputs of the low-dimensionnal model

### Datasets from Burgard et al, 2022
Burgard, C., Jourdain, N. C., Reese, R., Jenkins, A., and Mathiot, P.: An assessment of basal melt parameterisations for Antarctic ice shelves, The Cryosphere, 16, 4931–4975, https://doi.org/10.5194/tc-16-4931-2022, 2022.

- nemo_5km_isf_masks_and_info_and_distance_new_oneFRIS.nc (too big to be on GitHub)
- T_S_mean_prof_corrected_km_contshelf_and_offshore_1980-2018_oneFRIS.nc (too big to be on GitHub)
- nemo_5km_boxes_1D_oneFRIS.nc
- nemo_5km_boxes_2D_oneFRIS.nc

### Datasets from Nakata et al, 2021
Nakata, K., Ohshima, K. I., & Nihashi, S. (2021). Mapping of active frazil for Antarctic coastal polynyas, with an estimation of sea‐ice production. Geophysical Research Letters, 48(6), e2020GL091353.

- ice_prod_5km_Nakata.nc
- ice_prod_5km_Nakata_mean.nc

These datasets where obtained interpolating the original datasets on stereographic grids with 5km resolution

- bedmachine_5km.nc
